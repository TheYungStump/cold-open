## Short description (one line)

A free, open-source screenwriting app that opens and saves .fdx files, works offline, and never loses your work.

## Description

Cold Open is a free, open-source screenwriting app for Windows, macOS and Linux.

- Write in standard screenplay format: Enter and Tab move from scene heading to action to character to dialogue, and names, places and times of day complete themselves.
- Never lose work: every keystroke goes into a crash journal before it shows on screen, and the next start brings it back after a crash or power cut.
- Open and save Final Draft-compatible .fdx files; saving one back writes it as it came. Damaged .fdx files open with a repair report.
- Fountain, a plain-text screenplay format, is its own format.
- Track changes, production revision colors, a scene-by-scene draft comparison, a title page editor, and reports.
- PDFs laid out the same way on every computer.
- No account, no activation, no subscription; install it on every computer you have.
- Every action is also a command, from a command line, JSON over stdio, or an MCP server.

It's in alpha: careful with your work, but new.
