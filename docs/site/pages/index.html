A free screenwriting app that never loses your work

Cold Open is a free, open-source screenwriting app for Windows, macOS and Linux. Write in standard screenplay format, exchange .fdx files with producers, and keep every word, even through a crash.

Version {{version}}, an early alpha. No account, no subscription, no activation.

  • Free and open sourceEvery feature, for everyone, with no account, no trial and no subscription. The source is public, so it keeps working whatever happens to us.
  • Works offlineNothing to sign into and nothing to activate. Write on a plane, in a cabin, anywhere. Install it on every computer you have.
  • Never loses your workEvery keystroke goes into a crash journal before it shows on screen. If the app or your computer stops, the next start brings back everything you typed. Saves replace the file in one step, so a crash mid-save can't leave half a script.
  • .fdx exchange, byte for byteOpen the .fdx files producers send, and save them back as they came: our test files write back identical, byte for byte. A damaged .fdx opens with a report of what was repaired.
  • Windows, macOS and LinuxThe same app and the same page breaks on all three, including Linux, where Final Draft doesn't run. Every download is under 15 MB.
  • Writes like a screenwriting appEnter and Tab move from scene heading to action to character to dialogue. Names, places and times of day finish themselves. Fountain is its native format: plain text you can read anywhere.

Built for the way scripts get made

  • Revisions: track changes from collaborators and accept or reject each one, mark production revisions in color, and compare two drafts scene by scene.
  • Output: PDFs laid out the same way on every computer, a title page editor, and scene, character and location reports.
  • Your files: Fountain, .fdx and a Cold Open project that keeps everything. Nothing you write is locked in.

Cold Open is in alpha. It works, and it's careful with your work, but it's new. Keep a copy of anything that matters, and tell us what works and what doesn't.
