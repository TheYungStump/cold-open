# Cold Open

**A free, open-source screenwriting app.** It reads and writes Fountain and Final Draft–compatible `.fdx` files, lays out pages the same way on every computer, and never loses your work.

- **Write the way scripts are written.** Enter and Tab move from scene heading to action to character to dialogue, and names, places and times of day finish themselves.
- **Never lose a word.** Every keystroke goes into a crash journal before it shows on screen. If Cold Open or your computer stops, the next start brings everything back.
- **Exchange with everyone.** Open the `.fdx` files producers send and save them back as they came. A damaged `.fdx` opens with a report of what was repaired.
- **Revise like a production.** Track changes from collaborators and AI agents, accept or reject each one, mark production revisions in color, and compare any two drafts scene by scene.
- **Yours.** No account, no activation and no internet needed. Install it on every computer you have. It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux, and every download is under 15 MB.
- **Drive it any way you like.** Every action is a command, which you can also run from the command line, from your own programs, or through an AI agent over MCP.

> **Cold Open is in alpha.** It works, and it's careful with your work, but it's new. Keep a copy of anything that matters. Your scripts stay plain Fountain and `.fdx` files, so nothing you write is locked in.
