# What feedback helps

Any feedback helps. A few things make it much easier to act on.

## The most useful things

- **What you were trying to do.** "I wanted to move a scene to the end of act one" tells us more than "drag doesn't work".
- **What you expected, and what happened instead.**
- **The steps,** if you can make it happen again. "It happened once" is still worth telling us.
- **Where you got stuck, guessed, or had to look something up.** These are the rough edges we can't see ourselves.
- **Your version and system.** About Cold Open ▸ **Copy version info** copies them. **Report a problem…** fills them in for you.

## Files and scripts

- **Never send a script you don't own,** even a draft you were sent. Scripts are copyrighted, and issues are public.
- **Write a short scene of your own** that shows the problem. A few lines that go wrong are better than a whole script.
- **For an .fdx that won't open or saves wrong,** send the output of `coldopen repair your-script.fdx copy.fdx --json`. It lists what was wrong by code and position, and holds no script text.
- **Screenshots** help with how something looks. Crop out anything private or copyrighted.

## Crashes

If Cold Open crashed, the next start offers its crash report. **Look at it**, then **Send on GitHub** or **Send with the form**, and paste the report into the page that opens. It holds no script text. Tell us what you were doing when it happened, if you remember.

## Ideas

Tell us the problem before the solution: "I can't see which scenes are night scenes" lets us find the best answer, and maybe one you'd like better than your first idea.

## What's less helpful

- "It's slow", with nothing more. Which action, on what computer, with how long a script? **Measure performance** in the palette gives us numbers.
- Long lists of things another app does. Tell us which ones you'd miss most, and why.

Thank you for taking the time.
