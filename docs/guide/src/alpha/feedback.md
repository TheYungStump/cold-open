# Send feedback without an account

You don't need a GitHub account to tell us about a problem, a crash or an idea.

**[Open the feedback form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfuNuITmor09PTaR-_IX17x8w-yB3xDLDU9JgD6U3zyilyE7g/viewform)**. It takes a few minutes.

From Cold Open it's quicker: **Share feedback…** ▸ **Open the form**, or **Report a problem…** ▸ **Use the form** (both in the palette, About, and the start screen). The form opens with your text and the details filled in, and, if you tick it, your [usage summary](../guide/settings.md#the-usage-summary).

## How the form works

- **Your answers come to us privately.** Unlike GitHub issues, nobody else sees them.
- **It asks for nothing personal.** There's a box for a way to reach you, if you'd like a reply, and you can leave it empty.
- **From Cold Open:** **Report a problem…** ▸ **Use the form**, and the crash report's **Send with the form**, open it with Cold Open's version, your system and the renderer filled in, and nothing from your script.
- The form is a Google Form, so Google's [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies to what you type into it. You don't need a Google account.

Please don't paste a script you don't own. A short scene of your own that shows the problem is best. See [what feedback helps](what-helps.md).
