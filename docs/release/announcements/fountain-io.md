# The Fountain community: fountain.io's apps list

## Notes for the owner (not part of the message)

- **Where the community is:** fountain.io has an apps list (<https://fountain.io/apps>), with no forum, mailing list or chat linked from the site. The list asks whether you'd like your Fountain app featured, and points to a contact page on prolost.com, the site of one of Fountain's creators.
- **What to send:** a short, factual request to be listed. Send it through that contact page. Use a project address if the form asks for an email, not your personal one: until there's a project email, the form may not be the right channel. Hold it until you have one.
- **When:** after alpha.2 is out and the website is live, so the link works and shows a release.
- **Angle:** Fountain people care about the format being honoured. Cold Open's own format is Fountain, it follows the whole syntax page, and its Fountain library is MIT/Apache for other apps to use.

## The message

> **Subject:** A new Fountain app for the apps list: Cold Open
>
> Hello,
>
> I'd like to suggest Cold Open for the Fountain apps list. It's a free, open-source screenwriting app for Windows, macOS and Linux that uses Fountain as its own format.
>
> - It reads and writes the whole Fountain syntax page: title pages, dual dialogue, notes, boneyard, sections and synopses, and the lot. It keeps what it can't print, and never drops anything without asking.
> - It also opens and saves .fdx, so a Fountain writer can exchange with a production and stay in Fountain.
> - Its Fountain parser and writer are a separate library under MIT or Apache-2.0, for other apps to use.
> - It's in alpha. It's free, with no account, and GPL-3.0.
>
> Website: https://theyungstump.github.io/cold-open/
> Source: https://github.com/TheYungStump/cold-open
>
> Thank you for Fountain. It's the reason a project like this can exist.
>
> The Cold Open project
