## The post

**Title:** I made a free, open-source screenwriting app that reads and writes .fdx, and I'd like testers for the alpha

**Body:**

> I've been building **Cold Open**, a free, open-source screenwriting app, and its alpha is ready for people to try. I'd really value this sub's feedback.
>
> **What it does**
>
> - **Writes like a script:** Enter and Tab move from scene heading to action to character to dialogue, and names, places and times of day complete themselves.
> - **Never loses work:** every keystroke goes into a crash journal before it shows on screen. If the app or your computer dies, the next start brings it all back.
> - **Opens and saves .fdx** files the way they came, and repairs damaged ones with a report of what it fixed. Fountain is its own format.
> - **Track changes** with authors, accept or reject; **production revisions** with colored pages and asterisks; **compare drafts** scene by scene, moved scenes included; title page, reports and PDF export.
> - **Windows, macOS and Linux**, under 15 MB, no account, no activation, works offline, and sends nothing anywhere.
>
> **It's an alpha.** Everything above works, and it's careful with your work, but few people have used it yet, so keep copies of anything that matters. The builds aren't signed yet, so your system will warn you the first time; the site says what to click.
>
> **Why:** I wanted a writing app I could install anywhere, that never loses a word, and that I can't be locked out of.
>
> In the open: much of the code is written with AI coding assistants under my direction, and I review every change. The app uses AI only if you connect an assistant yourself. Nothing connects by default, and an assistant's edits come in as tracked changes you accept or reject.
>
> **What would help most:** try the 20-minute tester guide, or bring in a copy of a draft you own and tell me where it fell short. The page count against what you're used to is especially useful.
>
> - Website and download: https://theyungstump.github.io/cold-open/
> - Tester guide: https://theyungstump.github.io/cold-open/alpha/tester-guide.html
> - Feedback without a GitHub account: https://theyungstump.github.io/cold-open/alpha/feedback.html
>
> Please don't post or send scripts you don't own. A short scene of your own that shows a problem is perfect.
